# Design system — "professional newspaper, absurd journalism"

## Modes
* **Periódico** (public, default): cream paper `#F5E8CC`, dark ink `#251F1B`, a subtle SVG paper grain that is turned off in dark mode.
* **Redacción** (HQ): dark ink surfaces with gold `#D5AA4F` accents. Admins can toggle to Periódico, and the choice is remembered per browser.

Tokens live in `src/styles/index.css` (`:root` / `[data-mode]`) and are exposed to Tailwind via `@theme inline`.
Brand constants live in `src/branding/brand.config.ts`.

## Pastoral tone
`data-tone="pastoral"` re-scopes the tokens for La Voz del Pastor: lighter warm paper (`#F9F0DD`), soft gold rules, no breaking-red accent, larger serif body (`.pastoral-prose`, 1.8 line height), and an ornament divider. The same masthead and typography keep it consistent with the paper.

## Charts
The LP gain/loss pair was **validated with the dataviz palette validator**. The brand green/red pair failed
colorblind separation (ΔE 6.4), so charts use:

| | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| Gain | `#1F6FA8` | `#3B8FD0` |
| Loss | `#B5452B` | `#D9694C` |

Both modes pass the lightness, chroma, CVD (ΔE ≥ 18) and contrast checks. Every bar also carries ▲/▼ and a signed
value, missing data reads "s/d" (never 0), each chart has a tooltip on hover/focus, and a full data table is shown below.

## Rules
Legibility first. There are no fake scan artifacts. Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Focus rings are always visible.
Touch targets are at least 44px on public pages, and on-screen keyboard keys are 48px tall.
